# Open-Source CMOS PTAT Temperature Sensor with Digital Calibration

**ISSCC 2027 Code-a-Chip** — consolidated v13 project.

## Objective

A transparent, reproducible CMOS PTAT temperature-sensor study based on

`ΔVGS ≈ n·kT/q·ln(N)`

with digital one-point and two-point calibration, analytical/readout co-design, SKY130 transistor-level characterization, and explicit evidence provenance.

## Evidence classes

1. **Analytical** — first-principles Python model.
2. **Synthetic** — explicitly assumed Monte-Carlo variation; never presented as PDK/silicon.
3. **Real PDK** — generated by ngspice against SKY130/open_pdks and preserved with raw data, logs, SPICE decks, and provenance manifest.

### Power evidence boundary

The ideal-bias result is the power of the two forced-current sensing branches. The mirror result includes the characterized PMOS bias network. These are **testbench/core figures**, not a claim for complete sensor/ADC system power.

## Reproducibility

See `PDK_SETUP.md`, `Makefile`, `run_all.py`, `submission_preflight.py`, and `FINAL_VALIDATION_REPORT.md`.

The design seed is centralized in `design_requirements.json`. PDK evidence is cryptographically bound to that configuration; stale evidence is rejected if design requirements change.

## Important limitations

- The real PDK evidence is transistor-level simulation, not silicon measurement.
- The mirror sweep is deterministic corner/temperature characterization, not statistical mismatch Monte-Carlo.
- Layout, DRC, and LVS are not claimed as completed PASS.
- Calibration results must distinguish analytical/synthetic data from real PDK datasets.
